## Fases

| Fase | Propósito | Señales |
|---|---|---|
| Inform | Visibilidad y accountability | Allocation, reporting, anomaly detection, budgeting/forecasting |
| Optimize | Eficiencia por unidad de valor | Rightsizing, rate optimization, workload placement, unit cost |
| Operate | Gobierno continuo | Policies, automation, executive alignment, practice operations |

## Dominios → capacidades (atajo)

- **Understand Usage & Cost:** Data Ingestion, Allocation, Reporting & Analytics, Anomaly Management
- **Quantify Business Value:** Planning & Estimating, Forecasting, Budgeting, KPIs & Benchmarking, Unit Economics
- **Optimize Usage & Cost:** Architecting & Workload Placement, Usage/Rate Optimization, Licensing & SaaS, Sustainability
- **Manage the FinOps Practice:** Executive Strategy Alignment, Governance, Invoicing & Chargeback, Automation, Assessment, Intersecting Disciplines

## Madurez

- **Crawl:** procesos manuales, scopes limitados, ownership parcial
- **Walk:** procesos definidos, automation parcial, KPIs estables
- **Run:** automation amplia, scopes complejos (AI/SaaS/multi-cloud), influencia ejecutiva medible

## FOCUS

Especificación abierta para normalizar billing datasets entre vendors (cloud, SaaS, AI, etc.): https://focus.finops.org/

Denjiro exige datasets FOCUS-aligned cuando existan; si no, documenta mapping y no declare compliance.
